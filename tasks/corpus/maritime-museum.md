# Maritime Museum

The Maritime Museum is in Saltmarsh Landing. It opened in 1962 in the former customs house, a building that dates from 1858.

## Collection

The museum holds the original lens of Kestrel Point Lighthouse, ship models, and the logbooks of the first Harbor Ferry crews.

## Visiting

The curator is Daniel Abara. Admission is 9 dollars for adults and 4 dollars for children.

# Pinecrest Brewing Cooperative

The Pinecrest Brewing Cooperative was founded in 1994 by the siblings Nora and Felix Achterberg.

## Products

Its best-known beer is Kestrel Amber. The cooperative also brews Saltmarsh Stout and a seasonal Regatta Pale.

## Size

The cooperative has 42 employees and produces 11,500 hectolitres a year.

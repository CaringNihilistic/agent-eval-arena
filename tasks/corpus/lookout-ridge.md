# Lookout Ridge

Lookout Ridge is a village above Saltmarsh Landing, at the upper station of the Ridge Line funicular. It has a population of 2,140.

## Ridge Observatory

The Ridge Observatory stands at an elevation of 412 metres. It opened in 1968. Its main instrument, the Marlow reflector, has a mirror 1.2 metres across and is named after the observatory's founder, Edwin Marlow.

# Kestrel Point Lighthouse

Kestrel Point Lighthouse marks the northern entrance to Pinecrest harbour. The tower is 34 metres tall and was built in 1892.

## Operation

The light is visible for 19 nautical miles. It was tended by a resident keeper until 1978, when it was automated.

## The lens

The original lens was replaced in 1978 and is now on display at the Maritime Museum.

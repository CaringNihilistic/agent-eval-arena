# Saltmarsh Landing

Saltmarsh Landing is the largest town on Tidewater Bay, on the eastern shore. It was founded in 1846 and has a population of 31,250.

## Government

The mayor of Saltmarsh Landing is Helena Voss, in office since 2017.

## Institutions

The town is the seat of the Tidewater Transit Authority and of the Maritime Museum. The lower station of the Ridge Line funicular is at the top of Customs Street.

# Tidewater Bay Bridge

The Tidewater Bay Bridge carries the road between Saltmarsh Landing and Pinecrest. It opened in 2003 and is 2.9 kilometres long.

## Design

The bridge was designed by the engineer Ingrid Solheim.

## Tolls

The toll for a car is 3.50 dollars. Buses of the Tidewater Transit Authority cross free of charge.

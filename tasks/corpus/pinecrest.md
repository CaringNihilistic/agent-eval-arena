# Pinecrest

Pinecrest is a harbour town on the western shore of Tidewater Bay. It was founded in 1871 and has a population of 18,400.

## Government

The mayor of Pinecrest is Tobias Renner, who was first elected in 2019.

## Economy and events

The town is home to the Pinecrest Brewing Cooperative. Every August it hosts the start and finish of the Bay Regatta. Kestrel Point, with its lighthouse, lies four kilometres north of the harbour.

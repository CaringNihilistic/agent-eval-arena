# Harbor Ferry Service

The Harbor Ferry connects Pinecrest and Saltmarsh Landing. It began service in 1987 and is operated by the Tidewater Transit Authority.

## Schedule

Ferries leave Pinecrest every 40 minutes between 06:00 and 22:00. The crossing takes 25 minutes.

## Fleet

The fleet has three vessels. The newest, the MV Kestrel, entered service in 2019 and carries 310 passengers. The MV Heron and the MV Osprey each carry 240 passengers.

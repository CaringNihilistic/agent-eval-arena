# Bay Regatta

The Bay Regatta is a yacht race held every August on Tidewater Bay. It was first held in 1923.

## Course

The course runs from Pinecrest harbour to Kestrel Point and back, a distance of 14 kilometres.

## Results

The course record of 1 hour 52 minutes was set in 2019 by the yacht Grey Heron. The 2025 race was won by the yacht Second Wind, skippered by Marisol Etxeberria of Saltmarsh Landing.

## Sponsor

Since 2008 the regatta has been sponsored by the Pinecrest Brewing Cooperative.
